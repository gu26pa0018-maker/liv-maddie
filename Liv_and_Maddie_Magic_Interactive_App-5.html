01 • What is the show about?

The story follows identical twins who love each other but live very different lives.

👠 LIV ROONEY

  • A former Hollywood television star.
  • Confident, expressive and theatrical.
  • Returns to Wisconsin after her show ends.
  • Has to adjust from celebrity life to being back at home.

🏀 MADDIE ROONEY

  • A talented basketball player.
  • More athletic and grounded.
  • Stayed in Wisconsin while Liv was in Hollywood.
  • Has her own friends, school life and routines.

🎬 Main plot

The series is mainly about family, friendship, school, sports, relationships and growing up. The central challenge is learning how two very different sisters can share their lives again without losing what makes each of them unique.

02 • Episode 1 — “Twin-A-Rooney”

What happens?

Liv returns to Wisconsin after her television show ends. She is excited to be home, but her return changes the family's routine because she and Maddie have spent years living very different lives.

Maddie has built a life around school, basketball and her friends, while Liv is used to cameras, fame and Hollywood. Their reunion therefore creates awkward, funny and emotional moments as they try to reconnect.

The main idea

The first episode establishes the basic formula of the series: the twins are opposites in many ways, but their sisterly relationship remains important. The conflict comes mostly from adjusting to change and understanding each other's world.

03 • Crime connection

Liv & Maddie is not a crime TV show. However, one Season 1 episode includes a shoplifting storyline, which makes the series relevant to the assignment.

🕶️

SHOPLIFTING

In “Steal-A-Rooney” (Season 1, Episode 4), Kylie steals sunglasses while Liv is with her. Liv becomes associated with the theft even though she did not steal them.

⚠️

PEER PRESSURE

The storyline can be discussed through the pressure to participate in someone else's bad decision and the consequences of not being honest about what happened.

🤝

RESPONSIBILITY

The situation creates an opportunity to discuss accountability, telling the truth and supporting a friend without taking responsibility for a crime you did not commit.

Other crime types?

The series does not follow a recurring robbery, burglary, cybercrime, vandalism or detective-investigation format. Crime is occasional rather than the show's main genre.

04 • How would I make it right?

Keep the drama and humor — remove the illegal choice.

01 • Say no

Liv refuses to participate when Kylie suggests stealing something.

02 • Tell the truth

Liv explains what happened immediately instead of allowing confusion to grow.

03 • No false blame

Maddie supports Liv without taking responsibility for something she did not do.

04 • Positive resolution

The conflict becomes a lesson about honesty, boundaries, accountability and friendship.

My version of the episode

Kylie wants the sunglasses, but Liv notices what she is planning and stops her. Instead of stealing them, the girls find a legal way to get the same style. The episode can still have comedy and conflict, but the characters solve the problem without committing a crime.
